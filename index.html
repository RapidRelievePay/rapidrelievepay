Get treated today. Pay later, safely.

RapidRelievePay is an AI-powered WhatsApp assistant that pays a patient’s hospital bill directly, so no one has to delay or abandon care because they can’t pay upfront.

See how it works Partner with us
The problem

The bill arrives before the treatment does.

In Nigeria, the moment a patient is handed a bill they can't pay is often the moment treatment stops. Bank loans are too slow, loan apps charge excessive interest rates and often resort to crude recovery methods, and most people have no insurance..

76%

of health spending in Nigeria is paid out of pocket.

~10%

of Nigerians are covered by any form of health insurance.

95%

of Nigeria’s online population uses WhatsApp, the highest rate in the world.

How it works

Four steps, all inside WhatsApp.

1

Start a chat

At a partner hospital, the patient messages the RapidRelievePay assistant. No app to download.

2

AI intake

The assistant collects details and reads the hospital invoice, in English or Nigerian Pidgin.

3

Fast decision

A credit check built for Nigeria’s informal sector returns a decision in minutes.

4

Hospital paid directly

Funds go to the hospital, never the patient. The patient repays over 30–60 days with a flat, fixed fee.

What makes us different

Built for the moment of care.

WhatsApp-native

Meets people on the app they already use every day.

Hospital-direct payment

Money is purpose-locked to treatment, so it can’t be redirected.

Flat fee, no compounding interest

The total repayment is clear from day one.

Credit model for thin files

Weighs clinical urgency and hospital referral, not just bureau history.

Who we serve

Patients

  • Working Nigerians in the informal and lower-formal economy: traders, artisans, drivers, tailors, small business owners.
  • People without health insurance who face sudden medical bills.
  • Active on WhatsApp, but unlikely to download finance apps.
 

Hospitals & clinics

  • Private and mission hospitals that absorb bad debt or turn patients away.
  • Same-day payment for treatments that would otherwise be delayed.
  • No cost to join as a partner.
